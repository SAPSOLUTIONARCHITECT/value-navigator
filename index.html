Core capabilities

Everything you need to manage better.

Engage handles the small, time-consuming tasks and brings essential information to your fingertips — so you can focus on what truly matters.

AI-powered recruitment

Create, validate, and publish job openings with ease. Leverage AI for smart job descriptions, resume parsing, and candidate ranking to streamline hiring from day one.

Time and attendance

Track work hours, manage shifts and overtime, and integrate automatically with payroll. Improve accuracy and workforce productivity across every location and timezone.

Digital onboarding
